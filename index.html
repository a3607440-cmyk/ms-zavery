A little help before you choose.

How do I enquire about a design?

Tap “Enquire about this design”. WhatsApp opens with its name already included. Add your questions and send the message when you’re ready.

Why are prices not listed?

The price depends on the material, weight, stones, size and current rate. Ask us for the full price and availability of the exact design you like.

Are the AI designs finished jewellery?

AI design concepts and styling previews are inspiration visuals. They are labelled separately. Ask us to confirm what can be made or supplied, along with the final details.

Can you help with size or a visit?

Call or WhatsApp us to discuss ring or bracelet sizing. Please confirm the address and a suitable time directly with us before travelling.
